    Got Questions?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What makes zebra blinds different from regular blinds?

    +

    Zebra blinds use dual alternating sheer and solid fabric stripes. By aligning or misaligning the stripes, you control exactly how much light and privacy you want — a feature regular blinds do not offer.

    Can I get zebra blinds in custom sizes?

    +

    Absolutely. All our zebra blinds are custom made to your exact window measurements. We offer a free home measurement visit as well.

    Do zebra blinds provide blackout?

    +

    Standard zebra blinds filter light beautifully. We also offer blackout zebra blinds with a special backing fabric for rooms where you need complete darkness.

    Do you offer motorized zebra blinds?

    +

    Yes! We offer remote controlled motorized zebra blinds that are perfect for offices and modern homes. Contact us for pricing and availability.

    How do I clean and maintain zebra blinds?

    +

    Simply use a soft dry cloth or a light vacuum brush attachment to remove dust. Avoid wet cleaning as it can damage the dual fabric. We provide full care instructions with every order.

    Got Questions?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between real wood and faux wood venetian blinds?

    +

    Real wood blinds are made from natural basswood and offer a premium warm finish. Faux wood blinds are made from PVC composite — they are moisture resistant, more affordable, and ideal for kitchens and bathrooms.

    Can I get wooden venetian blinds in custom sizes?

    +

    Absolutely. All our wooden venetian blinds are custom made to your exact window measurements. We offer a free home measurement visit to ensure a perfect fit.

    What slat sizes are available?

    +

    We offer 25mm slim slats for a modern look and 50mm wide slats for a classic bold statement. Both are available in real wood and faux wood options.

    Are wooden venetian blinds suitable for humid areas like kitchens?

    +

    Real wood blinds can warp in high humidity. For kitchens, bathrooms, or humid areas we strongly recommend our faux wood venetian blinds which are 100% moisture resistant.

    How do I clean wooden venetian blinds?

    +

    Use a soft dry microfibre cloth or a vacuum brush to dust the slats. For faux wood, a lightly damp cloth also works. Avoid soaking real wood slats in water. Full care instructions are included with every order.

    Got Questions?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between fabric and PVC vertical blinds?

    +

    Fabric vertical blinds offer a softer, more elegant look with a wide range of colors and textures. PVC vertical blinds are moisture resistant, easier to clean, and ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and high-humidity areas.

    Can I get vertical blinds in custom sizes?

    +

    Absolutely. All our vertical blinds are custom made to your exact window or door measurements. We offer a free home measurement visit to ensure a perfect fit every time.

    Are vertical blinds suitable for large windows and sliding doors?

    +

    Yes! Vertical blinds are ideal for large windows, floor-to-ceiling glazing, and patio or sliding doors. The vertical vane design allows easy stacking to one or both sides for full access.

    Are vertical blinds suitable for humid areas like kitchens?

    +

    Our PVC vertical blinds are 100% moisture resistant and perfect for kitchens, bathrooms, and laundry rooms. Fabric vanes should be used in dry areas for best longevity.

    How do I clean vertical blinds?

    +

    Use a soft dry cloth or vacuum brush to dust the vanes. PVC vanes can be wiped with a lightly damp cloth. Fabric vanes can be spot-cleaned or removed and hand-washed. Full care instructions are included with every order.

    Got Questions?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do blackout blinds block 100% of light?

    +

    Our blackout blinds are made from specially coated fabrics that block 100% of light when properly fitted. For maximum blackout effect we recommend side channels or a close-fit installation which our team can advise on during the free measurement visit.

    Can I get blackout blinds in custom sizes?

    +

    Absolutely. All our blackout blinds are custom made to your exact window measurements. We offer a free home measurement visit to ensure a perfect, gap-free fit for maximum light blocking.

    Are blackout blinds good for bedrooms and nurseries?

    +

    Yes! Blackout blinds are ideal for bedrooms, nurseries, and any room where you need complete darkness. They help improve sleep quality and are especially popular for babies, shift workers, and home cinema rooms.

    Do blackout blinds also provide thermal insulation?

    +

    Our thermal blackout blinds feature an extra insulating lining that helps keep rooms cooler in summer and warmer in winter, reducing energy costs. Standard blackout blinds also provide a degree of insulation over bare windows.

    How do I clean blackout blinds?

    +

    Use a soft dry microfibre cloth or a vacuum brush attachment to dust the fabric. For spot cleaning use a lightly damp cloth with mild detergent. Avoid soaking or machine washing as this can damage the blackout coating. Full care instructions are included with every order.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a wooden box curtain cornice?

    +

    A wooden box cornice is a decorative frame mounted above your window, crafted from wood with hand-carved or CNC-cut artistic patterns. It hides the curtain rod and adds an architectural elegance to your room, creating a luxurious, finished look.

    Can I get custom wood carving designs?

    +

    Absolutely. We offer fully custom wooden box designs including arabesque, floral, geometric, and traditional Pakistani patterns. Share your idea or reference image and we will craft it to perfection.

    What fabrics are available for wooden box curtains?

    +

    We pair wooden box cornices with premium velvet, linen, sheer, blackout, and brocade fabrics. You can also add an inner sheer layer for a layered, luxurious look. All fabrics are available in a wide range of colors.

    How long does it take to make and install?

    +

    Custom wooden box curtains typically take 5 to 7 working days from measurement to installation. Rush orders can be accommodated — please contact us to discuss your timeline.

    Is installation included in the price?
